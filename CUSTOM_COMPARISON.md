# ✅ Custom Comparison & Trend Analysis - Live!

## New Capabilities

I've enhanced the Advanced Graph with powerful comparison tools:

### 1. Custom Comparison (Side-by-Side)
-   **Select Specific Items**: Compare "Ahmet" vs "Mehmet" or "Company A" vs "Company B".
-   **Multi-Select**: Search and select as many items as you want.
-   **Aggregated View**: Shows the total performance for the whole year for each selected item.

### 2. Monthly Trend Analysis (Time-Series) 📈
-   **New "Aylık Trend" Switch**: Turn this ON to see how performance changes **over time**.
-   **Visualize Trends**: 
    -   Instead of just a total bar, you see a **Line Chart** spanning the whole year (Jan-Dec).
    -   Each selected person/company gets their own colored line.
    -   Great for spotting who performed better in which month.
    -   *Example*: See if "Ahmet" had a better summer than "Mehmet".

### 3. Advanced Multi-Metric Visuals
-   **Mix Bars & Lines**: In Trend Mode, you can select multiple metrics (e.g., "Mal Bedeli" AND "KDV").
    -   **First Selected Metric**: Appears as **Bars**.
    -   **Other Metrics**: Appear as **Lines** overlaying the columns.
    -   This lets you visually check correlations (e.g., High Sales vs Low Tax) for each person simultaneously!

### 4. Smart Visualization
-   **Dosya Sayısı**: Appears as a **Red Line** on a right-side axis, solving scale issues.
-   **Mixed Charts**: Combine Bar and Line charts for flexible analysis.

## How to Use

1.  Go to **Gümrük** page > "**Gelişmiş Grafik Analizi**".
2.  Select a **Kategori** (e.g., "Giriş Elemanına Göre").
3.  **To Compare Totals**:
    -   Leave "Aylık Trend" **OFF**.
    -   Select items from the dropdown.
    -   Result: Side-by-side bars.
4.  **To See Trends**:
    -   Turn "Aylık Trend" **ON**.
    -   Select items from the dropdown.
    -   Check "Mal Bedeli" (shows as Bar) and "KDV" (shows as Line).
    -   Result: Detailed monthly breakdown.

Enjoy the new insights! 🚀
